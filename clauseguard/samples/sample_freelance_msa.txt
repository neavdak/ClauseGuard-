FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Freelance Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Growth Labs Pvt Ltd ("Company"), having its office in Gurugram, and Priya Nair, an independent graphic designer resident in Bengaluru ("Freelancer").

1. Definitions

"Deliverables" means all work product, drafts, concepts, source files, tools and materials created or conceived under this Agreement. "Services" means the design services described in any Statement of Work.

2. Services and Engagement

The Freelancer shall perform the Services described in Statements of Work issued by the Company. The Company may request revisions at any time, and acceptance of Deliverables shall be determined in the Company's sole discretion. The Freelancer shall be available during the Company's business hours and shall prioritise the Company's work over other clients.

3. Fees and Payment

Invoices shall be paid on Net 60 terms after the Company accepts the Deliverables. No late payment fee or interest shall apply to any delayed payment by the Company. If the Agreement is terminated for any reason, no payment shall be due for work in progress, and amounts previously invoiced for Deliverables not finally accepted may be forfeited. The Freelancer shall invoice all expenses with original receipts.

4. Intellectual Property

As between the parties and as work made for hire, the Freelancer hereby assigns to the Company, on a perpetual, irrevocable, exclusive, worldwide and royalty-free basis, all right, title and interest in and to all Deliverables, including all copyrights, design rights, moral rights and rights of attribution, together with all pre-existing tools, templates, fonts and generic methodologies used in creating the Deliverables. The Freelancer shall not display the Deliverables in any portfolio without the Company's prior written consent, which may be withheld in its sole discretion.

5. Confidentiality

The Freelancer shall keep all information shared by or on behalf of the Company confidential in perpetuity, without limit in time, and shall not use such information for any purpose during or after the term. This obligation extends to information that becomes public other than through breach, and to general skills and know-how acquired during the engagement. Breach shall entitle the Company to injunctive relief without proof of damage.

6. Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation

During the term and for five (5) years thereafter, the Freelancer shall not engage, directly or indirectly, in any design, branding or marketing services for any company that competes with the Company anywhere in India, and shall not solicit any customer, prospect, employee or contractor of the Company for any reason.

7. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date first written and continues until terminated. The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, on immediate notice, and shall have no further payment obligation beyond Deliverables accepted before termination. The Freelancer may terminate only on ninety (90) days' prior written notice and must complete all outstanding work during that period.

8. Indemnification

The Freelancer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Company and its officers from and against any and all claims, damages, losses and expenses (including legal fees), including unlimited, indirect, incidental and consequential damages, arising in any way from the Services or Deliverables, regardless of fault and even if caused in whole or in part by the Company's materials, instructions or negligence. The Company shall have no reciprocal indemnity obligation.

9. Limitation of Liability and Warranties

EXCEPT FOR THE INDEMNITY ABOVE, WHICH IS UNLIMITED, IN NO EVENT SHALL THE COMPANY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, OR FOR ANY DAMAGES EXCEEDING THE FEES ACTUALLY PAID IN THE MONTH OF THE CLAIM. All Services and Deliverables are provided "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE", and the Company disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including fitness for any particular purpose communicated by the Company.

10. Automatic Renewal and Price Changes

Statements of Work shall renew automatically for successive monthly periods unless cancelled seven (7) days before renewal. The Company may modify rates, scope or these terms at any time by email notice, and continued work constitutes acceptance of the modified terms.

11. Assignment and Subcontracting

The Company may assign or subcontract this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any third party, including in connection with a merger or asset sale, without consent or notice. The Freelancer may not subcontract or delegate any obligation without the Company's prior written consent.

12. Amendments and Waiver

The Company may amend, supplement or waive any provision of this Agreement at any time in its discretion; any such change is binding when posted to the Company's vendor portal. No course of dealing shall constitute a waiver of the Company's rights.

13. Personal Data

The Freelancer may receive personal data of the Company's customers and shall process it as instructed by the Company, keep it confidential, and delete or return it on request.

14. Security Deposit

As security for performance, the Freelancer shall deposit an amount equal to two months' fees with the Company. The Company may deduct sums from the deposit for any claimed deficiency without prior notice, and any remaining balance shall be returned within a reasonable time after termination.

15. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, pandemic measures, war and government action, provided the affected party gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate.

16. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Gurugram, Haryana, for all disputes, and the unsuccessful party shall bear the successful party's full legal costs.

17. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, with any Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, including email promises made during recruitment.
